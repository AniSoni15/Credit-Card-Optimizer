Before you enter spendingA few quick rules for accurate numbers
Walmart & Target

Most cards treat them as general retail and pay 1x. Put them in Everything Else, not Groceries.

Groceries

Only supermarkets count. Warehouse clubs and superstores usually don’t, so they belong in Everything Else too.

Rent

Most cards earn 0x on rent or can’t be used for it. Bilt is the main exception (0.5–1.25x), so only enter rent if you’d pay it by card.

Travel: portal vs. direct

Booking through a card’s own portal earns a different (often higher) rate than booking direct. Choose how you book in Preferences → Airlines.

Bills & the rest

Utilities, phone, insurance and anything without its own category go in Everything Else.

How your credit score is used

Every card has a researched minimum credit tier. Cards above your stated tier are left out of Your ideal lineup (never cards already in your wallet). It’s a guide, not an approval promise. What each setting does

“Keep it simple” caps Your ideal lineup at 2 cards unless you set “Max cards” in Rewards & fees above. This is also the one setting that leaves rotating-bonus cards (Freedom Flex, Discover it) out; otherwise they’re always included.

Card issuer rules & eligibility

Advisory only — this tool doesn't know your application history, so these aren't factored into any card's score. Worth checking before you apply:

  • Chase 5/24: Chase usually declines most of its cards if you’ve opened 5+ personal cards in the past 24 months.
  • American Express: Welcome offers are once per lifetime per card; Amex also limits how many cards you can hold.
  • Capital One: Informally limited to about 1 new personal card every 6 months, and roughly 5 Capital One cards total.
  • Bilt: Underwritten by Wells Fargo; standard Wells Fargo new-account pacing rules tend to apply.
  • Issuer rules change without notice — verify current terms directly with the issuer before applying.

Recommended order

How this is estimated

Order is by real gross $/mo contribution to Your ideal lineup. Chase cards move to the front only once you’ve logged 3+ personal cards opened in the last 24 months, since Chase typically declines at 5. Capital One is modeled at ~6 months between approvals; Amex’s once-per-lifetime welcome offer is flagged if you’ve logged that card; everything else gets a flat 3-month gap. None of these are issuer guarantees, and they change. When a card stops earning its keep, downgrade to a no-fee version rather than closing it, to protect your credit history (see Drop in My Wallet).

Data & methodology

Rates & values. Multipliers were researched from issuer terms in Aug 2026 and simplified; choose-your-own-category cards and welcome offers are noted per card but not fully modeled. Dollar values assume typical redemption rates (transferable points 1.6–1.8¢, airline miles 1.2–1.3¢, hotel points 0.5–1.5¢, cash back 1¢) — yours will vary. Preferences adjust these for you: your home hub’s miles go up, other airlines’ go down (all airlines if you never fly), chosen hotel chains go up, and “cash back only” caps points at 1¢. Custom categories are matched by keyword — adjust if it looks wrong.

Rotating quarterly categories. Chase Freedom Flex and Discover it pay an elevated rate on categories that change each quarter (capped, activation required). Their bonus counts in your earnings and totals whenever the card is activated in My Wallet, and Your ideal lineup always includes them (a 5% category beats almost any other rate) unless you choose “Keep it simple.” Because the categories change each quarter, your best setup can change too; the table at the bottom of My Wallet shows what each category reverts to. The shared cap is split in a fixed priority order, niche merchant or charity categories aren’t modeled, and unannounced quarters use the base rate.

Bilt 2.0 rent tiers. Blue, Obsidian and Palladium charge no fee on rent, but the rate slides from 0.5x to 1.25x with your other spend on the card that cycle (0.5x at 25% of rent, 0.75x at 50%, 1x at 75%, 1.25x at 100%). In your wallet it’s computed from your assigned categories; elsewhere it assumes the best-case 1.25x.
